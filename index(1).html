Safety: Stop in a safe place before using this map, and never look at your phone while riding. Pedestrians always have the right of way.

Prototype. Routes were traced by students and haven’t been checked on foot. Treat every route as “recommended,” never guaranteed safe.

Campus rules

  • Personal scooters must be registered with the university.
  • Do not block traffic or walkways.
  • 20 mph on campus roads, 10 mph in parking lots.
  • Scooters are allowed on sidewalks, plazas, and grass within reason.
  • Pedestrians always have the right of way but should not obstruct traffic.
  • Hands-free technology only to the extent allowed by law.

Summarized by the project team. Confirm against official UTSA policy.

Accessibility
